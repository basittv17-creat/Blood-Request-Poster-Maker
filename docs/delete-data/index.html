Request Data or Account Deletion

This is the official data deletion request page for Blood Request, published by Gc29.

How to request deletion

You can request deletion even if you no longer have the app installed. Send an email to awasikharal@gmail.com with the subject Blood Request - Data Deletion Request.

Tell us whether you want to remove a submitted WhatsApp donation-group link, its associated Firebase anonymous account/data, or an administrator account. To help us locate a group submission, include its displayed group name or the invite link if available. If requesting an administrator account deletion, contact us from the email address associated with that account where possible. We may ask for minimal verification before deleting data that belongs to an account.

Do not send passwords, patient names, identity documents, medical records or other sensitive information. We will review your request, confirm the information needed to identify the data and respond through email. The public website itself does not automatically delete Firebase records.

Data that may be deleted

  • Donation-group submissions: Pending or approved group names and WhatsApp invite links associated with a request, once identified.
  • Firebase Authentication: An identifiable anonymous or administrator authentication account and related records controlled by the app developer, following appropriate verification.
  • On-device drafts: Delete individual drafts in Blood Request or use the app's Privacy controls to clear local drafts. Clearing the app's storage removes locally stored drafts and settings.

What may remain and for how long

Pending group requests remain until reviewed or removed; approved group names and links remain until moderators remove them. The app does not automatically delete a Firebase Authentication account when a group is removed. We handle matching deletion requests individually rather than according to a fixed automatic deletion period.

Posters exported to your device must be deleted by you from the saved location. Files or information already shared with WhatsApp or another service are handled under that service's policies; we cannot delete those copies for you. Firebase and other service providers may retain limited technical, security or compliance records under their own retention policies.

For more details about local storage and Firebase processing, read the Blood Request Privacy Policy.

Contact

Email awasikharal@gmail.com to start a deletion request or ask a privacy question.
