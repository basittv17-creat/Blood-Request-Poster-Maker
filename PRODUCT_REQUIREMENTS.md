# Product requirements

- Android-first offline poster creation and editing.
- First-launch language choice and overflow language switching for Urdu, Hindi, and English.
- Patient, hospital, location, two phones, and one of eight ABO/Rh groups. Optional urgency, date, ward, units, notes, dialing code, donor instructions, and custom title and footer.
- Twelve selectable designs, favorite marking, nonrepeating shuffle, and persistent drafts.
- PNG and JPEG at 1920 × 1080, Android document save, and Android sharing.
- A verified official download URL is required for invitation sharing. Fictional examples display a demo badge and require confirmation before export.
