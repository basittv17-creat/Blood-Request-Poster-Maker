Privacy Policy

Blood Request is published by Gc29. This policy applies to the Android app and its donation-group directory. Effective November 24, 2026.

Posters and drafts

Poster fields can contain patient, blood group, hospital, location, and contact details you enter. The app uses this information on your device to render posters. It stores drafts, language settings, and favorites in private app storage. Poster content does not go to the donation-group directory or Firebase through the poster editor.

Saved PNG and JPEG files go to the location you choose in Android. If you share a poster, the app makes a temporary copy available to the app you choose. The recipient app handles that copy under its own policy. Get permission before sharing another person's details and include only what is needed.

WhatsApp groups and Firebase

The online directory reads approved group names and invitation links from Cloud Firestore. Submitting a group sends its name and link to Firestore for moderator review and uses Firebase Authentication to create an anonymous account. The associated Firebase user ID identifies the pending submission. Approved names and invites are public. Pending requests are available to authorized moderators. Do not put patient or personal contact information into a group submission.

Moderators sign in using email and password through Firebase Authentication. Firestore rules use account IDs and role records to restrict moderation. Google processes information needed to provide and secure its Firebase services, which may include IP addresses and user agents. Google may process information outside your country. See Firebase privacy and security.

Selecting Join opens an invite through Android in WhatsApp or another handler. WhatsApp or your browser then handles the request under its own privacy policy. The app does not automatically send your poster, contact list, or Firebase ID with the invite.

Retention and deletion

Delete drafts individually or use the app's Privacy menu to delete them all. Clear app data to remove local drafts and preferences. Delete exported copies from the place where you saved them. Copies already shared must be managed with the recipient or destination service. Temporary share files are cleaned up on later shares when older than an hour.

Pending documents are removed when approved or rejected. Published names and links remain until removed by a moderator. Removing a group does not automatically delete a Firebase Authentication account. The app has no in-app control to delete cloud records. Use the Data Deletion Request page or email Support to request removal of a group or deletion of associated account data. Give only the information needed to identify the request. Google documents separate retention for its technical and authentication records.

Children and safety

The directory is intended for adults who organize or support donation requests. A parent or guardian can contact Support about information involving a child. Avoid including a child's name or contact details in a publicly shared poster unless an appropriate guardian has authorized it and the disclosure is necessary.

Contact and updates
