# Privacy

The app stores drafts and settings in the Android app's private preferences with backups disabled in the manifest. It has no network permission in the main manifest, login, analytics, or location access. The Android save picker writes to the destination selected by the user. When sharing, the Android chooser grants the selected app access to a cached image. Older temporary images are removed on later shares after an hour. Saved files and copies retained by external apps are controlled outside this app. Delete drafts individually or use the Privacy menu to delete all drafts.
